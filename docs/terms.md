## 1. The service

OBot is a Discord bot that checks a member owns a Minecraft account, through the Discord account
linked on their Hypixel profile, then gives them roles and a nickname from their Hypixel rank
and guild. A web panel lets admins configure it.

The service is free. OBot's code is open source, under the BSD 3-Clause license: that license

instance is the responsibility of whoever hosts it.

Only people who have, in a server, the Administrator permission, or both Manage Server and
Manage Roles, can configure it from the panel.
